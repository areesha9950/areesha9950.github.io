Areesha Tahir

My work focuses on capturing real life moments and everyday subjects that are often overlooked. I’m interested in photography and digital media, using framing and editing to shift how something familiar is seen and felt. I approach my work through experimentation and careful attention to detail, allowing each piece to develop in a way that feels intentional while still open to unexpected outcomes. I am interested in exploring ideas of identity, beauty standards, and the tension between perfection and imperfection, as well as how the past and present exist together in a single moment. A sense of nostalgia runs throughout my work, reflecting my interest in memory and the feeling of holding onto moments as they pass. Through this, I aim to create work that feels warm and familiar, encouraging viewers to slow down, reflect, and find meaning in the ordinary details of their own lives.
